## Executive summary

Your workshop currently uses **AUTOMATE** ([garagemanagement.in](https://garagemanagement.in/)) at **₹12,000 per year** (~₹1,000/month). That is a competitive price for a full commercial garage platform.

This document compares both options on **cost**, **features**, and **fit**, so you can choose what works best for your shop.

## When to keep AUTOMATE

- You are happy with how the system works today  
- You use (or plan to use) customer app, inspections, CRM, or multi-branch  
- ₹12,000/year is acceptable and support from KAPTAS is good  
- You prefer not to manage software or hosting  

**Recommendation:** If nothing is broken, **staying on AUTOMATE is the most economical choice.**

---

## When to switch to JG GaragePro

- You only need **job cards, inventory, and billing** — not the extra modules  
- You want **your own data** and no vendor lock-in  
- You need **quick custom changes** (invoice layout, fields, reports)  
- AUTOMATE feels **slow, cluttered, or hard to train staff on**  
- You want **direct support** from someone who knows your shop  

**Recommendation:** Switch if daily workflow and ownership matter more than the lowest subscription price.
